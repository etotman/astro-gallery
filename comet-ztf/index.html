C/2022 E3 (ZTF)

The green comet of early 2023, stacked on the comet's nucleus so the stars streak past it.

Acquisition

RASA 8 and ASI533MC Pro, 30–60 s subs over three nights (31 January – 4 February 2023). Which subs went into this stack is not recorded.
